About this kit
This kit is designed for Lawful Permanent Residents navigating the transition to U.S. citizenship. Created by an immigrant who personally went through the process, it provides practical guidance, checklists, and trackers to help you stay organized and move confidently through your naturalization journey.

Based on FY 2025 processing times, the full journey can take 3 to 6 years from receiving a green card. We'll help you navigate every step.
