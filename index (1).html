From the founder

"The process is confusing, expensive, and designed without you in mind. This kit changes that."

Landed was built for Lawful Permanent Residents navigating the path to U.S. citizenship — by someone who personally went through every step. It gives you practical checklists, guides, and tools to stay organized and move through naturalization with confidence.
